# Shelfline — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Bank product manager

- As a product manager, I want to publish a lending SKU with max volume, price floor, and adjudication mode, so that partners cannot originate beyond our risk appetite.
- As a product manager, I want to see contribution margin by distributor, so that I scale partners that create economic value, not just applications.

### Partnerships lead

- As a partnerships lead, I want distributor contracts with branding and complaint-ownership fields, so that experience ownership is negotiated before go-live.
- As a partnerships lead, I want to pause a distributor in one action when fraud spikes, so that we do not wait on engineering to cut risk.

### Treasury / ALM analyst

- As a treasury analyst, I want hard caps on partner-sourced deposits by tenure band, so that franchise value and liquidity coverage are not accidental.
- As a treasury analyst, when inflows approach the cap, I want automatic SKU throttling, so that we do not discover the breach in the month-end ALM pack.

### Distributor developer / product owner

- As a distributor, I want a stable catalog API of approved SKUs and rate cards, so that I can embed bank products in my UX without emailing a banker.
- As a distributor, I want clear error codes when adjudication is bank-side pending, so that my customers are not left in a silent spinner.

### Conduct / platform administrator

- As a conduct officer, I want evidence of who owned the customer conversation for a mis-sold partner product, so that redress is not a finger-pointing exercise.
- As a platform administrator, I want an audit log of SKU publishes, price changes, and pauses, so that internal audit can reconstruct who changed manufacturing terms.
- As a credit risk officer, when multi-platform leverage data shows a borrower already saturated, I want partner origination blocked, so that unbundled lending does not hide total exposure.
